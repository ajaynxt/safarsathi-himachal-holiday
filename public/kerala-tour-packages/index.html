A southern escape with hills and backwaters

Safar Sathi lists a 5-night, 6-day Kerala route idea connecting Cochin, Munnar, Thekkady and Alleppey. The itinerary combines hill-station sightseeing, a spice-country stop and a backwater houseboat experience. Travel times between destinations can be longer than they look on a map, so keep the schedule realistic and confirm houseboat check-in times, meal plans and hotel categories before booking.

Suggested 5-night, 6-day route

Use this as a starting point. The final route, travel time, inclusions and sightseeing availability should be confirmed in your quotation.

  1. 01Day 1: Arrive at Cochin and transfer to Munnar.
  2. 02Day 2: Munnar sightseeing with tea-estate viewpoints.
  3. 03Day 3: Drive to Thekkady and explore the local area.
  4. 04Day 4: Continue to Alleppey for a backwater houseboat experience.
  5. 05Day 5: Travel towards Cochin and explore as time permits.
  6. 06Day 6: Departure from Cochin.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Kerala trip include an Alleppey houseboat?

Yes. Ask for an overnight or day-cruise option and confirm the boat category, route, meals, check-in and checkout timings.

Is a private cab available throughout the Kerala route?

You can request a private vehicle quotation for the full route. Confirm the vehicle type, driver allowance, tolls, parking and any state-specific charges.

Can we add more nights in Munnar?

Yes. The itinerary can be adjusted to allow a slower stay or additional sightseeing, depending on your overall travel dates.
