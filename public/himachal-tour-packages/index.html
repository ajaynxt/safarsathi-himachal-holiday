ROUTE INSPIRATION

A popular 8-night Himachal circuit

Use this as a starting point. The final route, travel time, inclusions and sightseeing availability should be confirmed in your quotation.

  1. 01Day 1–2: Arrive from Delhi or Chandigarh and explore Shimla and Kufri.
  2. 02Day 3–4: Travel to Manali via Kullu; plan Solang Valley and Atal Tunnel subject to conditions.
  3. 03Day 5–6: Continue towards Dharamshala and explore McLeodganj.
  4. 04Day 7–8: Visit Dalhousie and Khajjiar before the return transfer.
BEFORE YOU BOOK

Frequently asked questions

Useful details to help you plan with clarity.

What is a good duration for a Himachal holiday?

A Shimla–Manali trip can be planned in around 5 nights and 6 days. Adding Dharamshala or Dalhousie needs more time. The best duration depends on your pickup city and travel pace.

Can I start my Himachal tour from Delhi or Chandigarh?

Yes, enquire about pickup and drop options from Delhi or Chandigarh. Confirm the exact route and transfer timings with the team.

Is Spiti suitable throughout the year?

Spiti is a high-altitude destination. Access and road conditions vary by season. Confirm current route status, acclimatisation needs and suitable travel dates before booking.
