Destination guide

Shimla and Manali

A classic Himachal road journey with hill-town sightseeing, Himalayan viewpoints and the Kullu Valley. Route choice and daily pace depend on pickup city, travel dates and road conditions.

Ideas to discuss: Explore Shimla’s Ridge and Mall Road; discuss onward travel through Kullu to Manali; consider optional nearby sightseeing only after checking weather and local access.

Spiti Valley

A high-altitude Himalayan region known for dramatic landscapes, monasteries and remote villages. Plan around acclimatisation, road access, weather and enough travel days.

Ideas to discuss: Discuss the Shimla–Kinnaur or Manali-side approach; allow gradual altitude gain; consider Kaza, Key Monastery and nearby villages subject to current access.

Destination guide

Leh and Ladakh

A high-altitude destination with long drives, mountain passes and changing weather. Build a realistic plan around acclimatisation and current road conditions.

Ideas to discuss: Keep initial days light; discuss Nubra Valley and Pangong Lake routing; check permits, road access and local requirements before departure.

Destination guide

Rajasthan

Historic cities, forts, palaces, markets and desert landscapes. Group nearby destinations rather than trying to cover the entire state in a short trip.

Ideas to discuss: Explore Jaipur heritage sights; add Jodhpur, Udaipur or Jaisalmer only when the trip length allows; check monument timings.

Jammu and Kashmir

Lakeside experiences, gardens and mountain destinations. Travel times and access vary by season, weather and local conditions.

Ideas to discuss: Explore Srinagar and Dal Lake; discuss Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg according to season; check local vehicle and sightseeing rules.

Kerala

Hill stations, backwaters, beaches and cultural experiences. The best route depends on arrival city, trip duration and preferred pace.

Ideas to discuss: Consider Munnar and Alleppey; discuss boat or stay type separately; check monsoon conditions and activity schedules.

Uttarakhand

Hill towns, Himalayan viewpoints, forests and pilgrimage destinations. Mountain drives can take longer than expected, so route planning matters.

Ideas to discuss: Discuss Mussoorie, Rishikesh, Nainital or Auli based on trip length; check seasonal access and allow realistic road-time buffers.

Goa

North and South Goa offer different coastal experiences, from lively beach areas to quieter stretches and heritage sites.

Ideas to discuss: Choose a base around preferred beaches; discuss heritage areas and transfers; check seasonal weather and activity availability.

North East India

A diverse region spanning several states, with long intercity journeys and some state-specific entry requirements. Plan one region at a time.

Ideas to discuss: Discuss Assam and Kaziranga subject to season; consider Meghalaya destinations such as Shillong and Cherrapunji; check permits and current conditions.

A common starting point for journeys towards Shimla, Manali and other North Indian destinations. Pickup details and departure timing help make a realistic plan.
