BEFORE YOU BOOK

Frequently asked questions

Essential guidance to ensure a safe and memorable Ladakh journey.

Why is Day 1 rest mandatory in Leh?

Leh is located at an altitude of over 11,500 feet (3,500 meters). Flying in directly from the plains causes sudden exposure to low oxygen. Spending the first 24 hours resting helps avoid Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS).

What vehicle types are available for the Ladakh circuit?

For high passes and rocky terrain, we deploy sturdy 4x4 and high-ground-clearance vehicles such as Toyota Innova Crysta, Scorpio, and Force Tempo Travellers driven by experienced mountain chauffeurs.

Are meals and stay included in the tour package?

Yes, packages typically include hotel and luxury Swiss cottage camp accommodation with breakfast and dinner (MAP plan), private vehicle for all sightseeing, and airport transfers.
