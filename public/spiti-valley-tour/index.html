A THOUGHTFUL START

Plan a Spiti journey with the right preparation

Safar Sathi lists a 7-night, 8-day Spiti circuit idea connecting Kinnaur, Tabo, Dhankar, Kaza, Key Monastery and the high villages around Komic, Hikkim and Langza. Some itineraries also include Chandratal and Manali, depending on seasonal access. Spiti is a high-altitude region: build in acclimatisation time, consult a medical professional if you have health concerns, carry essential medicines and verify road and weather conditions before departure.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Frequently asked questions

Useful details to help you plan with clarity.

What is the usual season for a Spiti road trip?

The listed circuit is generally planned for the warmer travel window, but opening dates and conditions vary. Verify current local road status before confirming.

Is a regular sedan suitable for Spiti?

Vehicle suitability depends on the exact route, season, road condition and group requirements. Request a vehicle recommendation from the operator for your specific itinerary.

How should I prepare for the altitude?

Plan gradual ascent and rest, stay hydrated, avoid overexertion initially and learn the symptoms of altitude illness. Seek medical advice before travelling if you have relevant health concerns.
