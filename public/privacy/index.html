Privacy notice

This website helps you request travel, hotel and cab quotations. Enquiry forms prepare a message for WhatsApp; sending that message is your choice and does not confirm a booking.

Information and analytics

Travel dates, contact details and other information you choose to include in a message are shared with the travel team when you send it. Optional Google Analytics measures visits and contact intent. This setup does not intentionally send enquiry form contents as custom analytics events. Use Privacy preferences on the main website to choose or withdraw analytics permission.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Google and other external websites have their own privacy practices. Google privacy policy · WhatsApp privacy policy.
